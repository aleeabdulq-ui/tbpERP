# TBP ERP — What the App Will Do for TBP

Prepared for: The Information Service Manager, The Building Practice Limited

## One place to manage everyday work
TBP ERP will bring agreed departmental processes into one application. Staff will be able to record their work, submit requests, follow approvals and find the information they are authorised to use. Management will be able to see progress, outstanding work and agreed financial reports without relying on separate updates from every department.

## What the app will do
| Area | What TBP will be able to do |
|---|---|
| 1. Secure access | Give each user access suited to their duties, restrict confidential records and keep a history of important actions. |
| 2. Management dashboards | View agreed summaries of projects, outstanding tasks, approvals and departmental activity. |
| 3. Human resources | Keep employee records together and manage leave, attendance, training and performance reviews using TBP-approved policies. |
| 4. Tasks and accountability | Assign work to named staff, set deadlines, track submissions, give feedback and record work returned for correction. |
| 5. Projects and documents | Track projects and milestones; organise drawings and revisions; record site updates and requests for information. |
| 6. Time and project costs | Record and approve project time, apply Accounts-approved cost rules and report project costs and estimated profitability from approved inputs. |
| 7. Finance and payroll | Maintain agreed invoice, payment, expense, payroll and budget records, and show outstanding receivables and agreed financial summaries. |
| 8. Procurement and stock | Track suppliers, purchase requests, approvals, purchase orders, goods received and stock movements. |
| 9. Assets and administration | Record asset assignments, vehicle bookings, facilities maintenance and agreed administrative activities. |
| 10. IT support and reporting | Track support requests, IT records and renewals; share internal messages and produce agreed reports. |

The application will support these activities through connected records and controlled access. It will not replace TBP’s responsibility to approve expenditure, financial rules, personnel decisions or professional project work.

# What TBP Stands to Gain

## Practical benefits for the business
- **A clearer management picture:** See what is happening across departments and identify work that needs attention.
- **Better accountability:** Know who owns each task, when it is due and whether it has been reviewed or approved.
- **Less repeated administration:** Reduce avoidable re-entry and searching across separate records, spreadsheets and messages.
- **Better project-cost visibility:** Bring approved staff time and other project costs together to support pricing, planning and financial review.
- **Stronger control over spending and stock:** Follow the history from a purchase request through approval, receipt and stock movement.
- **More organised staff and company records:** Keep employee, project, asset and support information in a consistent structure.
- **Clearer access and approval records:** Restrict sensitive information and make important decisions easier to trace.
- **Long-term use:** Under the proposed perpetual licence, TBP can continue using the delivered version without recurring per-employee or per-department licence fees. Hosting and optional support remain separate.

## Can we achieve this?
**Yes. We can deliver the proposed TBP ERP capabilities through a planned implementation, based on a scope, timetable and responsibilities agreed with TBP.** This is a statement of the proposed delivery capability, not a claim that every feature is already complete.

We will confirm the requirements, complete and configure the agreed functions, test the connected workflows, and present the application for TBP’s review in a test environment. Following written acceptance, readiness confirmation and the agreed final payment, we will deploy the application, train the agreed users and hand over the operating guidance.

## What we need from TBP
TBP will need to nominate decision-makers, provide authorised records, confirm its workflows and participate in testing. Its Accounts department must supply or approve financial inputs, rates, calculation methods and reporting rules. We will implement those approved rules rather than assume TBP’s internal policies.

## How TBP will know it works
Authorised representatives will test the agreed functions against approved examples. Material defects will be corrected and retested. Deployment, training and handover will then be verified separately. The proposed 90-day warranty starts on written production acceptance.

**The intended result:** a practical system that helps TBP organise its work, follow decisions and make better-informed management choices. Benefits depend on accurate data and consistent use; no particular saving, profit increase or delivery date is guaranteed by this summary. Scope, dates, payment, licensing and support obligations remain subject to the final written agreement. This summary supports, but does not replace or amend, the budget proposal.
